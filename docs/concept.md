Concept: Why Data Mirror?

In the digital age, data collection is often invisible.

Most users are not aware of:

what data is being collected
how it is used
what consequences it may bring

This invisibility creates a gap between technology and human understanding.

Data Mirror is designed to make the invisible visible.

Instead of explaining digital rights through abstract concepts, this project translates everyday digital behaviors into tangible insights.

It is not only a technical project, but also an educational tool.
